Selective excitation and readout

The lab studies waveform design for coupled superconducting circuits, crosstalk suppression, and quantum nonreciprocity, connecting circuit models with physical device constraints.

Quantum Technologies and Metamaterials Lab (QTM Lab), Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Florida International University, 10555 W Flagler Street, Engineering Center, Miami, FL 33174. Email akrasnok@fiu.edu.

Supported by NSF, DOE, AFOSR, ONR, and the Intelligence Community Postdoctoral Research Fellowship Program, with computing from NVIDIA and IonQ. Part of the FIU Quantum Initiative.
