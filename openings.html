Join the QTM Lab

We welcome Ph.D. students, postdocs, and undergraduates from electrical engineering, physics, optics, or computing. Each project combines modeling with experiments.

How to apply · Fellowships · Contact

How to apply

Email akrasnok@fiu.edu a CV, a transcript, a short research statement, and any papers, code, or technical writing you have. Say which position you are asking about (Ph.D., postdoc, undergraduate, or visiting), when you could start, and which topics interest you.

Openings depend on funding, project fit, and mentoring capacity. Research assistantships, university fellowships, and external awards can support graduate study, and we discuss funding with each applicant. Students from all countries are welcome to ask.

Research areas

  • Complex-frequency electrodynamics and non-Hermitian scattering
  • Quantum control, circuit QED, and cryogenic quantum interfaces
  • Metamaterials, metasurfaces, antennas, and nanophotonics
  • Quantum sensing, MRI metasurfaces, and applied metrology

How we work

Your own project

Students help shape their projects, starting from the literature and their interests, and get regular feedback on calculations, experiments, and manuscripts.

Reproducible work

Versioned Python and Jupyter code, traceable data, and documented measurements let others check and build on each result.

Training

New members learn the equipment, measurement methods, and FIU safety rules, along with technical writing and publication-quality figures.

Next career step

We support fellowship applications, conference talks, and careers in academia, industry, or national laboratories, including after members move to new positions.

Fellowships

We help applicants prepare fellowship proposals: email a CV, your research interests, and the program you plan to apply to, with its deadline. Most national deadlines fall between October and January, and each sponsor’s page has current dates and eligibility.

  • NSF Graduate Research Fellowship (GRFP): three years of support; U.S. citizens, nationals, and permanent residents
  • NDSEG Fellowship: three years for defense-related science and engineering; U.S. citizens and nationals
  • Hertz Fellowship: up to five years of Ph.D. support in applied science, mathematics, or engineering; U.S. citizens and permanent residents
  • DOE SCGSR: 3 to 12 months of thesis research at a DOE laboratory for Ph.D. candidates who are U.S. citizens or permanent residents
  • GEM Fellowship: graduate funding with paid industry or national-laboratory internships; U.S. citizens and permanent residents
  • DOE Computational Science Graduate Fellowship: for early-stage Ph.D. students who use high-performance computing or develop computational methods; U.S. citizens and permanent residents
  • FIU Graduate School fellowships, including dissertation fellowships; some need a department nomination
  • FIU DOE Fellows: applied research and training, with work at DOE sites and national laboratories
  • SMART Scholarship: tuition, stipend, and internships, with civilian service after graduation
  • Optica scholarships and travel grants for optics and photonics students
  • NASA FINESST: graduate research in Earth and space science; see the program page for the next call
  • FIU scholarships, including awards open to international students
