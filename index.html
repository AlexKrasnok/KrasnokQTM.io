We study how shaped electromagnetic waves and engineered materials control light, sense weak signals, and address quantum devices.

The Quantum Technologies and Metamaterials Lab (QTM Lab) is part of the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Florida International University (FIU) in Miami. We combine theory, numerical modeling, and experiments in photonics, metamaterials, microwave systems, superconducting quantum hardware, and cryogenic sensing.

Prof. Aleksandr Krasnok leads the lab. He co-founded the FIU Quantum Initiative and teaches FIU's quantum-computing courses.
