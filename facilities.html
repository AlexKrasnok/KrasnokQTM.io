Facilities and equipment

Optical, microwave, and cryogenic measurements at FIU, combined with simulation and experiments at partner laboratories.

What we measure and model

Optics and photonics

Lasers and broadband sources from the ultraviolet to the mid-infrared, fiber and polarization optics, detectors, spectrometers, and Raman and optical microscopy.

Microwave and RF

S-parameter measurements from 50 MHz to 20 GHz, oscilloscopes, waveform generators, and lock-in detection for resonators, antennas, and metamaterials.

Cryogenics

A Montana Instruments CryoAdvance cryostat with a magneto-optic module for low-temperature optical and sensing experiments.

Electromagnetic simulation

Frequency- and time-domain full-wave solvers, reduced models, and GPU and HPC parameter sweeps.

Quantum computing

Qiskit, QuTiP, Python, and Jupyter for teaching and modeling, IonQ cloud access, and 32,000 NVIDIA A100 GPU-hours in January–June 2026. FIU’s own IonQ quantum computer is expected in late 2027.

Partners

FIU shared facilities and external partners support fabrication, characterization, and computing. Work with RadiaBeam, the University of Chicago, Argonne, Simon Fraser University, and LAPLACE in Toulouse connects our models with superconducting-resonator and plasma experiments.

Instruments

Montana Instruments CryoAdvance

Cryogenic platform with magneto-optic module for low-temperature optical and sensing experiments.

Nikon Eclipse Ti2-E Microscope

Inverted research microscope for optical inspection and imaging.

Renishaw inVia Raman Microscope

Raman spectroscopy and microscopy for materials, nanophotonics, and sensing studies.

8720ES Network Analyzer

S-parameter measurements from 50 MHz to 20 GHz for RF, microwave, and resonator characterization.

Siglent SDS1202X-E Oscilloscope

200 MHz two-channel digital oscilloscope.

SDG1032X Function Generator

Arbitrary waveforms for pulse, modulation, and control experiments.

Signal Recovery 7270 Lock-in Amplifier

Digital lock-in amplifier for measuring weak signals.

Genesis MX532 Laser System

Water-cooled single-frequency 532 nm laser.

Safeaire Fume Hood

Fume hood for sample preparation.

Equipment list

Optics

Irises, fiber patch cables (320–2200 nm), fiber collimators (532 to 980 nm), variable beamsplitters, Rochon prisms, achromatic lens kits, Glan-Thompson and Glan-Taylor polarizers, and apochromatic microscope objectives.

Light sources and lasers

Broadband sources covering 240–3800 nm, a 520 nm nanosecond pulsed laser diode, HeNe lasers, a Genesis MX532 single-frequency laser, and laser-safety equipment.

Detection and spectroscopy

Photodiode power sensors, an integrating sphere, a power and energy console, avalanche photodetectors, a scanning-slit beam profiler, and a fiber spectrometer (200–1100 nm).

Microscopy and cryogenics

Montana Instruments CryoAdvance with magneto-optic module, Nikon Eclipse Ti2-E microscope, Renishaw inVia Raman microscope, two Newport Integrity 2 VCS optical tables, and enclosures.

Electronics and RF

8720ES network analyzer (50 MHz–20 GHz), Siglent SDS1202X-E oscilloscope, SDG1032X and UTG932E generators, Keithley 2182A nanovoltmeter, Signal Recovery 7270 lock-in amplifier, filters, and mounts.

Teaching kits

Michelson interferometer, quantum-cryptography analogy, and quantum-eraser demonstration kits.
