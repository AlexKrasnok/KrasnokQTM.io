Prof. Aleksandr “Alex” Krasnok

Assistant Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Florida International University (FIU) and Director of the Quantum Technologies and Metamaterials Lab (QTM Lab).

Resume · 2-page PDF

Research and leadership

Alex Krasnok studies complex-frequency electrodynamics and nanophotonics and applies them to quantum control, sensing, and the diagnostics of quantum hardware. He tests analytical models and computer simulations against experiments on resonant systems at optical and microwave frequencies and at cryogenic temperatures.

He joined FIU in August 2021 after appointments at CUNY, UT Austin, and ITMO University. His papers include work in Science, Nature, and Physical Review Letters.
