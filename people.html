People

We study quantum and electromagnetic systems through theory, computation, and experiments. Students and postdocs own their projects, write their own papers, and get support for their next career step.

Prof. Aleksandr Krasnok

Assistant Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Florida International University and director of the QTM Lab, with a courtesy appointment in the Knight Foundation School of Computing and Information Sciences. His research covers complex-frequency electrodynamics, quantum control, photonics, sensing, and quantum-hardware diagnostics. He co-founded the FIU Quantum Initiative and is a core member of FIU's university-wide quantum committee.

Collaborating groups

We work with Arjuna Madanayake's group at FIU, Andrea Alù's group at CUNY, Sergey Kutsaev at RadiaBeam, Andrew Cleland at the University of Chicago, and Michael Kelly at Argonne National Laboratory in the United States; D. M. Broun at Simon Fraser University and Leonid Belostotski at the University of Calgary in Canada; and the Sokoloff, Pascal, and Callegari team at LAPLACE, Toulouse (CNRS), in France. Joint projects combine circuit and electromagnetic models with superconducting-resonator, optical, and plasma experiments.
